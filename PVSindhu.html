P.V Sindhu

Field: Badminton

Country: India

Key Trait: P.V. Sindhu is famous for being a powerful, aggressive player on the court and a very humble, kind person who helps others off the court.

Description: Widely considered one of India's greatest athletes of all time, P.V. Sindhu is the first Indian woman to win two consecutive individual Olympic medals and holds the historic distinction of being the country's first and only badminton World Champion.

Widely considered one of India's greatest athletes, P.V. Sindhu transformed badminton through her fierce determination and elite physical reach. Born into a sports-centric family, she began her journey at age eight under coach Pullela Gopichand, famously traveling 56 kilometers daily for rigorous training. Her immense discipline paid off rapidly, catapulting her into the global top 20 by age 17. She made history in 2013 as the first Indian woman to secure a World Championship singles medal, laying the foundation for her legendary status as a two-time Olympic medalist and India’s first-ever badminton World Champion.
