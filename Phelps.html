Michael Phelps

Field: Competitive Swimming

Country: United States

Key Trait: Relentless Discipline & Mental Visualization

Description: Widely considered the greatest swimmer of all time, Michael Phelps holds the record for the most Olympic gold medals (23) and total Olympic medals (28) in history.

Started competitive swimming at age 7 to deal with ADHD and focus issues. Qualified for his first Olympic Games in Sydney 2000 at just 15 years old, becoming the youngest male swimmer to make an American Olympic team in nearly 70 years.

"You can't put a limit on anything. The more you dream, the farther you get."
— Michael Phelps
