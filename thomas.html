Dr. Tessy Thomas

Field: Aerospace Engineering & Defense Technology

Country: India

Key Trait: Precision engineering, strategic leadership, and resilience

Description: Renowned Indian aerospace engineer and former Director General of Aeronautical Systems at DRDO, known as the "Agni Putri" (Daughter of Fire).

Dr. Tessy Thomas was born in Alappuzha, Kerala, in 1963. Named after Mother Teresa, she grew up near the Thumba Rocket Launching Station, which sparked her fascination with rockets. Earning her B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from Thrissur Engineering College and M.Tech in Guided Missiles from IAT Pune, she joined DRDO in 1988 under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. She rose to become Project Director for the Agni-IV and Agni-V intercontinental ballistic missile systems, becoming the first woman engineer in India to lead a missile project.

"Science has no gender. Knowledge and hard work are the only things that count."
— Dr. Tessy Thomas
