Marta Vieira da Silva

Sport: Football

Country: Brazil

Key Trait: Unmatched dribbling ability, explosive flair, and creative step-over skill.

Description: Widely considered one of the greatest women football players of all time, Marta Vieira da Silva holds the world record for the most World Cup goals scored with 17 goals and is a six-time FIFA World Player of the Year in the womens category.

Marta Vieira da Silva’s legendary journey began on the dusty streets of Dois Riachos, Brazil, where she played barefoot. Facing intense gender discrimination from boys who refused to play with her, she persevered, eventually getting scouted by Vasco da Gama at age 14. Her international breakthrough came in Sweden with Umeå IK, launching her to global prominence. She became a transformative icon, capturing an unmatched six FIFA World Player of the Year titles and scoring a record 17 World Cup goals. Cementing her club legacy, she recently captained the Orlando Pride to their historic first NWSL Championship.
