Muhammad Ali

Sport: Boxing

Country: United States

Key Trait: Lightning speed, footwork, and social conviction

Description: Iconic heavyweight boxing champion and global cultural figure.

Muhammad Ali, born Cassius Clay in Louisville, Kentucky, began training at age twelve after his bicycle was stolen. He rose quickly through amateur ranks, winning a light heavyweight gold medal at the 1960 Rome Olympics. Turning professional, he shocked the boxing world by defeating Sonny Liston in 1964 to win the world heavyweight title, subsequently converting to Islam and adopting his new name. Stripped of his title in 1967 for refusing military draft induction, Ali returned after a three-year exile to participate in historic bouts like the "Rumble in the Jungle" and "Thrilla in Manila."

"Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee."
— Muhammad Ali
