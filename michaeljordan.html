Michael Jordan

Sport: Basketball

Country: United States

Key Trait: Unmatched competitive drive, vertical leap, and clutch shooting

Description: Legendary American basketball player who popularized the NBA worldwide.

Michael Jordan grew up in Wilmington, North Carolina, famously cut from his high school varsity basketball team as a sophomore. Channeling that rejection into legendary motivation, he hit the game-winning shot for the University of North Carolina in the 1982 NCAA Championship. Drafted third overall by the Chicago Bulls in 1984, Jordan transformed the franchise into a powerhouse. Overcoming physical postseason barriers against the Detroit Pistons' "Bad Boys," he led the Bulls to two distinct championship "three-peats" in the 1990s, elevating basketball into a global cultural phenomenon before retiring as an iconic athlete and team owner.

"I've failed over and over and over again in my life. And that is why I succeed."
— Michael Jordan
