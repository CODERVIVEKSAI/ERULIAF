Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Field: Aerospace Engineering

Country: India

Key Trait: Visionary leadership, humility, and dedication to youth empowerment

Description: Celebrated Indian aerospace scientist and 11th President of India, affectionately remembered as the "Missile Man of India."

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, in 1931. Coming from a modest background, he distributed newspapers as a child to support his family while pursuing his education. He studied aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology and joined DRDO and ISRO, where he directed India's first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-III) project. After leading the development of indigenous ballistic missiles and playing a key role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests, he served as the President of India from 2002 to 2007, spending his later years inspiring millions of students until his passing in 2015.

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep."
— Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
