Hedy Lamarr

Field: Applied Physics & Invention

Country: Austria / United States

Key Trait: Creative problem-solving, dual talent, and inventive foresight

Description: Austrian-born Hollywood actress and self-taught inventor who co-created frequency-hopping spread spectrum technology.

Hedy Lamarr was born Hedwig Eva Maria Kiesler in Vienna, Austria, in 1914. Rising to international fame as a Hollywood film actress during cinema's Golden Age, she pursued a quiet parallel passion for engineering and tinkering in her home laboratory. During World War II, concerned about Allied radio-guided torpedoes being jammed by Axis forces, she partnered with composer George Antheil to develop a frequency-hopping communications system. Granted a U.S. patent in 1942, her underlying principles laid the technological foundation for modern wireless communications before her death in 2000.

"All creative people want to do the unexpected."
— Hedy Lamarr
