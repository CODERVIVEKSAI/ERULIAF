Neymar Jr

Sport: Football (Soccer)

Country: Brazil

Key Trait: Creative flair and unpredictable skill

Description: Dynamic Brazilian winger renowned for deceptive agility and style.

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior began playing street futsal in Mogi das Cruzes before joining the Santos FC youth academy. Debuting professionally at age seventeen, he led Santos to victory in the 2011 Copa Libertadores, earning South American Footballer of the Year honors. Transferring to FC Barcelona in 2013, Neymar formed the legendary 'MSN' attacking trio alongside Lionel Messi and Luis Suárez, capturing a treble in 2015. In 2017, he completed a record-breaking transfer to Paris Saint-Germain, winning multiple domestic trophies before moving to Saudi Pro League club Al-Hilal in 2023 to continue his global career.

"I'm not a man who likes to lose. I always want to win."
— Neymar Jr
