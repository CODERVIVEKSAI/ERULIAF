Ronda Rousey

Sport: Mixed Martial Arts & Professional Wrestling

Country: United States

Key Trait: Fierce competitive drive, elite judo mastery, and pioneering combat dominance

Description: American professional wrestler, author, and former mixed martial artist and judoka who became the first female UFC champion.

Ronda Rousey was born in Riverside, California, in 1987. Overcoming childhood speech apraxia, she followed in her mother's footsteps to excel in judo, becoming the youngest American judoka to qualify for the 2004 Olympics and winning a bronze medal at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. Transitioning to mixed martial arts, she dominated Strikeforce before becoming the first UFC Women's Bantamweight Champion in 2012. After a legendary MMA career, she successfully transitioned into WWE, capturing world championships and continuing to break barriers in combat sports entertainment.

"I'm not a ready-made princess. I'm a warrior. I'm strong, I'm proud, and I'm my own person."
— Ronda Rousey
