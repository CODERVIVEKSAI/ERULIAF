Pelé

Sport: Football (Soccer)

Country: Brazil

Key Trait: Vision, heading accuracy, and anticipation

Description: Legendary Brazilian football icon widely hailed as "The King."

Edson Arantes do Nascimento, known worldwide as Pelé, grew up in poverty in Três Corações, Brazil, practicing football with rolled-up socks stuffed with rags. Signed by Santos FC at age fifteen, he made his international debut for Brazil at sixteen. In 1958, at just seventeen years old, Pelé took the FIFA World Cup by storm in Sweden, scoring a hat-trick in the semifinals and two goals in the final. Spending nearly two decades with Santos before joining the New York Cosmos to promote soccer in North America, Pelé became global football's first world-renowned ambassador.

"Success is no accident. It is hard work, perseverance, learning, studying, sacrifice and most of all, love of what you are doing."
— Pelé
