Usain Bolt

Sport: Running

Country: Jamaica

Key Trait: Massive stride length and explosive power

Description: Widely considered the greatest sprinter of all time, Usain Bolt holds the world records for the 100-metre at 9.58s and 200-metre at 19.19s dashes and is an eight-time Olympic gold medalist.

From a cricket loving Jamaican boy battling scoliosis and frequent hamstring injuries, Usain Bolt overcame his physical limitations to become the greatest sprinter in history. Discovered by local coaches who noticed his raw speed on the school playground, he transitioned to track and field and became the youngest World Junior Champion at age fifteen. Under the guidance of coach Glen Mills, Bolt strengthened his core, refined his technique, and rose to global superstardom at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. He went on to capture eight Olympic gold medals and set unbreakable world records, transforming his natural gift into an historic, legendary legacy.
