Dr. Narinder Singh Kapany

Field: Fiber Optics & Applied Physics

Country: India / United States

Key Trait: Optical innovation, entrepreneurial spirit, and pioneering vision

Description: Indian-born American physicist widely celebrated as the "father of fiber optics" for demonstrating high-speed light transmission through flexible glass fibers.

Dr. Narinder Singh Kapany was born in Moga, Punjab, India, in 1926. After graduating from Agra University, he moved to London to complete his PhD at Imperial College London in 1955. There, he conducted groundbreaking experiments demonstrating that light could be guided through flexible glass fibers via total internal reflection. Moving to Silicon Valley, Kapany worked as a researcher, professor, and entrepreneur, founding multiple optics companies and publishing over one hundred scientific papers before his death in 2020.

"When you take light and bend it, you change the way the world communicates."
— Dr. Narinder Singh Kapany
